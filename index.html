Meet TeeJaye

A storyteller.
A believer.
A work in progress.

I’m TeeJaye DeLoach, a romance author and visual storyteller based in Las Vegas.

I write LGBTQ+ characters who are allowed to be complicated, hopeful, flawed, faithful, funny, scared, and deeply loved. I’m drawn to found family, second chances, and the courage it takes to live honestly.
