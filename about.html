My story

Stories have always been how I make sense of the world.

I’m TeeJaye DeLoach, a romance author and visual storyteller based in Las Vegas. My fiction centers LGBTQ+ characters who are allowed to be complicated, hopeful, flawed, faithful, funny, scared, and deeply loved.

I’m drawn to second chances, found family, questions of identity, and relationships that require people to show up honestly. Some stories touch faith directly. Others simply carry the same belief underneath them: people can grow, healing is possible, and love can change the direction of a life.

When I’m not writing, I’m usually behind a camera, thinking about travel, enjoying Disney or Star Trek, building a playlist, or drinking something caffeinated while imagining another fictional problem for my characters.
