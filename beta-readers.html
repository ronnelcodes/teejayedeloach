What to expect

A reader’s eye on a finished manuscript.

Beta reading is about how the story feels and works for you as a reader. It is not proofreading or professional editing.

What you’ll receive

Selected readers will receive private, revocable access to the advance digital manuscript of Borrowed for the Holidays through BetaBooks. A free BetaBooks reader account is required.

How feedback works

You’ll read online in BetaBooks and share feedback in one organized place, including chapter reactions and answers to reader questions.

Feedback I’m looking for

Characters and chemistry, pacing, emotional impact, continuity, confusing moments, and anything that especially worked—or did not work—for you.

The commitment

Read the manuscript by October 4, keep the unpublished story confidential, and provide candid, respectful feedback.
